The 4D Framework

1

Delegation

Decide what work to do yourself, what to give to AI, and how to split it. Understand the goals, the task, and what the AI can and cannot do.

Should AI do this, and which part?
2

Description

Communicate clearly: the product you want, how the AI should approach it, and how it should behave. Good context and prompts drive good results.

Have I said what I need, and how?
3

Discernment

Evaluate what the AI produced and how it got there. Check accuracy, quality, and fit with your goals before relying on it.

Is this right, and is it good enough?
4

Diligence

Take responsibility for the outcome: choose tools thoughtfully, be transparent about AI involvement, and own what you share.

Am I using and presenting this responsibly?
Description → Discernment → Refine Description ↻
The description-discernment loop: prompt, assess, improve, repeat.

Three Ways to Interact with AI

A

Automation

You define the task; AI executes it.

B

Augmentation

You and AI work on it together, thinking in partnership.

C

Agency

AI acts independently on your behalf, within the behavior you set.

Key Takeaways

Fluency goes beyond "cool prompts": it is a durable skill set.
The framework applies regardless of which AI model or tool emerges.
Delegate strategically instead of reactively.
Always evaluate output critically before using it.
Be transparent about AI involvement; humans stay accountable.
Useful across creative, business, and educational contexts.
